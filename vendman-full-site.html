1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you submit a quote request or contact form. This may include your name, company name, email address, phone number, emirate and any details you share about your location or requirements.

We may also automatically collect limited technical information — such as browser type, device type and general usage data — to help us understand how the website is used and to keep it secure.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to quote requests and enquiries, and to arrange site surveys or installations
  • To provide, maintain and improve our machines, products and services
  • To communicate with you about your account, request or an existing installation
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud or misuse

3. Payments at our machines

Cashless payments made at Vendman machines (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay and tap & go) are processed by Nayax and other regulated third‑party payment providers. Vendman does not receive or store your full card details — these are handled directly by the payment processor under its own security standards and privacy terms.

4. Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies and similar technologies to ensure the site functions correctly. We do not use cookies for third‑party advertising.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who support our operations (such as payment processing, IT hosting or logistics), and where required by UAE law or a valid legal request.

6. Data retention

We retain the information you provide for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry, deliver our services, or as required by law, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you about a request, by emailing us at the address below.

8. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy
